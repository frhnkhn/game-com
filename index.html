GAMECOM SESSIONS

MONDAY

Casual Gaming

Relax and play your favorite multiplayer games with the community.

Play. Relax. Connect.

Casual Gaming Sunday is GameCom's weekly hangout where members come together to play games, have fun, meet new people, and enjoy some friendly competition.

No pressure. No rankings. Just games, laughs, and good company.

🎮 What Happens?

  • 🕹️ Casual Game Sessions
    Play a variety of multiplayer and party games with fellow GameCom members.
  • 👥 Play With the Community
    Join existing groups, meet new players, and team up with people you haven't played with before.
  • 🏆 Friendly Competition
    Take part in small challenges, mini-tournaments, and friendly matches without the pressure of competitive esports.
  • 🎲 Game Variety
    From multiplayer games and party games to co-op experiences, the games can change every week.
  • 💬 Hangout & Connect
    Use the session to chill, talk about games, share recommendations, and get to know other members.

🔥 Weekly Activities

Multiplayer game nights, Party games, Co-op challenges, Mini tournaments, Community game picks, Random team battles, Game recommendations, Fun challenges.

👥 Community Focus

The goal isn't just to play games — it's to bring the GameCom community together.
Everyone can participate, whether you're a hardcore gamer, a casual player, or someone trying a game for the first time.

🎮 Session Flow

  • 01 — Pick the Games: Members vote or suggest games for the week.
  • 02 — Form Teams: Players are grouped into teams or lobbies.
  • 03 — PLAY: Jump in, compete, cooperate, and have fun.
  • 04 — COMMUNITY: Share highlights, funny moments, and experiences from the session.

“No pressure. No grind. Just games, good vibes, and the GameCom community.”

WEDNESDAY

Community Session

Skill-building workshops, creative learning, and community collaboration.

Learn. Share. Grow Together.

Community Session Wednesday is GameCom's weekly learning space where members share their knowledge, discover new tools, and learn practical skills from fellow members.

From Canva and Blender to Unity, Unreal Engine, game development, design, and other creative technologies, every session is an opportunity to learn something new.

📚 What Happens?

  • 🎓 Skill-Based Sessions
    Members conduct sessions on tools, technologies, and skills they know and want to share with the community.
  • 🛠️ Hands-On Learning
    Instead of just watching, members get the opportunity to follow along and try things themselves.
  • 💡 Explore New Tools
    Discover software, workflows, techniques, and technologies that can help you in your projects.
  • 👥 Learn From Each Other
    GameCom members can take turns becoming the instructor and sharing what they've learned.
  • 💬 Ask & Discuss
    Sessions encourage questions, discussions, and sharing different approaches to solving problems.

🔥 Possible Session Topics

🎨 Canva & Graphic Design, 🧊 Blender & 3D Modeling, 🎮 Unity, 🕹️ Unreal Engine, 🌐 Web Development, 💻 Programming & Development, 🎬 Video Editing, 🎨 UI/UX Design, 🤖 AI Tools & Workflows, 🎵 Game Audio, 🧩 Game Design

🧑‍🏫 How It Works

  • 01 — Choose a Topic: A member or team selects a skill/tool they want to teach.
  • 02 — Learn Together: The session introduces the basics and demonstrates the workflow.
  • 03 — Hands-On Practice: Members follow along and create something themselves.
  • 04 — Share & Discuss: Everyone can ask questions, share their work, and exchange ideas.

🌱 Community Focus

The goal isn't just to teach a tool — it's to create a culture where members teach, learn, experiment, and grow together.

“Everyone has something to learn. Everyone has something to teach.”

SUNDAY

Technical Session

Hands-on learning, technical exploration, and project development.

Learn. Build. Experiment.

Technical Session Monday is GameCom's dedicated space for turning technical knowledge into practical projects and real-world skills. Members work with different technologies, build projects, solve problems, and collaborate with others to bring ideas to life.

🛠️ What Happens?

  • 💻 Technical Workshops
    Explore technologies, development tools, frameworks, and workflows used in game development and creative technology.
  • 🚀 Project Development
    Members work on individual or team-based projects and turn concepts into working prototypes and applications.
  • 🧩 Hands-On Problem Solving
    Tackle technical challenges, debug issues, experiment with different approaches, and learn by building.
  • 👥 Team Collaboration
    Develop projects together across different roles such as programming, game development, UI/UX, 3D, design, and technical art.
  • 🔬 Experiment & Explore
    Try new technologies and ideas that members may not have worked with before.

🔧 Possible Technologies

💻 C / C++ / C#
🐍 Python
🌐 HTML, CSS & JavaScript
⚛️ React & Web Development
🗄️ Firebase & Backend Development
🐙 Git & GitHub
🤖 AI & Machine Learning
🧠 Generative AI & AI Tools
🐳 Docker & Development Environments
☁️ Cloud & Deployment

📋 How It Works

  • 01 — Choose the Project: Members select an existing GameCom project or propose something new.
  • 02 — Assign Roles & Tasks: Tasks are divided among members based on their interests and skills.
  • 03 — Build & Develop: Teams work on their assigned tasks and continuously improve the project.
  • 04 — Test & Solve: Members test their work, identify problems, and work together on solutions.
  • 05 — Showcase Progress: Teams share what they've built, what they've learned, and what comes next.

🎯 The Goal

Technical Session isn't just about learning another programming language or tool. It's about building something real, gaining practical experience, and developing skills that members can carry into their own projects, portfolios, and future opportunities.

Dev Play

February 2026

Build under pressure. Create without limits.

🚀 DEVPLAY’26

Build Under Pressure

DevPlay’26 was a 6-hour, team-based engineering arena designed to challenge participants to build real, functional applications under time pressure.

Rather than focusing only on writing code, the event challenged teams to demonstrate problem-solving, system design, technical decision-making, teamwork, and execution.

⚡ THE CHALLENGE

Participants worked in teams to design and develop an application within a strict 6-hour development window.

Teams had to move quickly from an idea to a working solution while dealing with changing requirements, limited time, and technical constraints.

The challenge:

Think → Design → Build → Adapt → Deliver

💻 TECHNOLOGY DOMAINS

Participants could build solutions across multiple technology domains, including:

  • 💰 FinTech
  • 🏥 HealthTech
  • 🤖 Artificial Intelligence
  • 🎮 GameTech
  • ⛓️ Blockchain
  • 🌐 Web & Software Development
  • And other technology-driven domains

This allowed teams to choose problems that matched their technical strengths and creativity.

👥 TEAM FORMAT

Team Size: 2–4 Members
Duration: 6 Hours
Format: Team-Based Engineering Challenge

Teams were encouraged to combine different skill sets, including:

Developers, Designers, Problem Solvers, System Architects, AI/ML Enthusiasts, Product Thinkers

🔥 EVENT STRUCTURE

DevPlay’26 followed a competitive, phase-based format designed to simulate real-world development environments.

  • 01 — IDEATE: Understand the challenge, identify the problem, and formulate a solution.
  • 02 — ARCHITECT: Plan the system, define the technical approach, and divide responsibilities.
  • 03 — BUILD: Turn the idea into a functional application within the given timeframe.
  • 04 — ADAPT: Respond to changing requirements and constraints while continuing development.
  • 05 — SUBMIT: Complete the solution and submit the final working application.
  • 06 — PRESENT: Explain the solution, technical decisions, and implementation to the evaluation panel.

🧠 WHAT WAS EVALUATED?

DevPlay’26 went beyond simply asking “Does the code work?” Teams were challenged on:

  • 💡 Problem-solving
  • 🏗️ System design
  • ⚙️ Technical implementation
  • 🔄 Adaptability
  • 🤝 Team collaboration
  • ⏱️ Execution under time constraints
  • 🧠 Technical reasoning
  • 🎤 Clarity of presentation
  • 🚀 Overall solution quality

🏁 EVENT DETAILS

  • Event: DevPlay’26
  • Theme: Build Under Pressure
  • Duration: 6 Hours
  • Date: February 28, 2026
  • Time: 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM
  • Team Size: 2–4 Members

🚀 THE DEVPLAY EXPERIENCE

DevPlay’26 wasn't just about building software.

It was about making decisions when time is limited, requirements evolve, and every technical choice matters. Teams had to collaborate, adapt, solve problems, and turn ideas into functional applications before the clock ran out.

6 Hours. One Challenge. Build Under Pressure.

🎮 GAMECOM ESPORTS

Compete. Connect. Conquer.

GameCom Esports brought together competitive gamers for an action-packed gaming experience featuring some of the biggest titles across tactical shooters, battle royales, fighting games, and football.

From intense tactical rounds to last-player-standing battles and head-to-head matchups, participants competed, connected, and represented their skills across multiple games.

  • 📱 BGMI: Battle Royale competition where teams fought for survival, positioning, and the coveted Chicken Dinner.
  • 🔥 FREE FIRE: Fast-paced Battle Royale matches testing quick decision-making, reflexes, and survival skills.
  • 👊 TEKKEN: One-on-one fighting battles where players went head-to-head with their characters and combat skills.
  • ⚽ FC 26: Competitive football matches where players battled it out on the virtual pitch for supremacy.

⚡ THE EXPERIENCE

The event featured a mix of team-based and individual competitions, giving players multiple ways to compete. Participants experienced:

  • 🎮 Competitive matches
  • 🏆 Tournament-style gameplay
  • 🤝 Team coordination
  • ⚔️ Head-to-head battles
  • 🔥 High-pressure moments
  • 🎉 Community interaction
  • 🥇 Bragging rights

🎯 EVENT HIGHLIGHTS

  • MULTIPLE GAMES: Five different titles brought together players from different gaming communities.
  • COMPETITIVE ACTION: Every match demanded focus, strategy, teamwork, and quick decision-making.
  • COMMUNITY: Players weren't just competing against each other — they were meeting fellow gamers and becoming part of the GameCom community.
  • CHAMPIONS: The best-performing players and teams battled through their respective competitions to claim the top spots.

🎮 GAMES. PLAYERS. COMPETITION.

GAME JAM

August 2026

24 hours of game development, and endless creativity.

🎮 24-HOUR GAME JAM

24 Hours. One Game. Endless Creativity.

A high-energy game development challenge where creators come together to turn ideas into playable experiences — all within 24 hours.

The GameCom 24-Hour Game Jam is designed to challenge participants to think creatively, work as a team, solve problems under pressure, and build a complete game from the ground up.

🎯 EVENT OVERVIEW

The 24-Hour Game Jam is an intensive game development event organized by GameCom, bringing together programmers, game designers, artists, developers, and gaming enthusiasts.

Participants form teams and spend 24 hours designing, developing, testing, and polishing a game based on the theme revealed at the beginning of the jam.

From the first concept to the final playable build, every decision counts.

⚡ THE CHALLENGE

You have 24 hours to:

  • 💡 Come up with an original game concept
  • 🎮 Design engaging gameplay
  • 💻 Develop a playable game
  • 🎨 Create or implement visual assets
  • 🔊 Add sound and music
  • 🧪 Test and improve your game
  • 🚀 Submit your final build before the deadline

The goal isn't just to make a game — it's to make the most of 24 hours.

👥 TEAM DETAILS

Team Size: 2–4 Members

Build your team with people who bring different skills to the table. You can collaborate with:

Game Developers, Programmers, Game Designers, 2D/3D Artists, UI/UX Designers, Sound Designers, Story Writers

No matter your role, everyone contributes to the final experience.

🛠️ WHAT CAN YOU USE?

Participants can use the tools and technologies they're comfortable with.

  • Game Engines: Unity, Unreal Engine, Godot, Other game engines
  • Development: C / C++, C#, GDScript, Python, JavaScript
  • Creative Tools: Blender, Photoshop, Canva, Figma, Krita, Other creative tools

Use whatever helps you bring your idea to life.

🔥 HOW IT WORKS

  • 01 — THEME REVEAL: The theme is announced at the beginning of the Game Jam.
  • 02 — IDEATE: Brainstorm, plan your gameplay, and decide what you're going to build.
  • 03 — BUILD: Start developing your game and turn your idea into a playable experience.
  • 04 — TEST: Playtest, debug, improve and polish your game.
  • 05 — SUBMIT: Submit your final game before the 24-hour deadline.
  • 06 — SHOWCASE: Games are evaluated and showcased to the GameCom community.

🏆 JUDGING

Games can be evaluated across multiple aspects, including:

  • 🎮 Gameplay & Fun
  • 💡 Creativity & Originality
  • 🎨 Art & Visual Design
  • 🔊 Audio & Presentation
  • 🧠 Innovation
  • ⚙️ Technical Implementation
  • ✨ Theme Integration
  • 🏁 Overall Experience

📦 SUBMISSION

Each team submits a playable build of their game along with the required project information. Your submission can include:

Game Build, Game Title, Short Game Description, Team Members, Controls / Instructions, Credits, Screenshots or Gameplay Video

🎮 WHAT YOU'LL EXPERIENCE

24 hours of: Brainstorming, Building, Breaking things, Fixing them, Testing, Laughing, Creating, And probably a lot of caffeine. ☕

Whether you're an experienced developer or participating in your first Game Jam, the event is about learning, experimenting, collaborating, and creating something you're proud of.

🎮 READY TO BUILD?

24 hours. One theme. One team. One game.

Think fast. Build smart. Create something unforgettable.
